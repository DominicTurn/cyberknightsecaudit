01 / Privacy

Information We Collect

Cyber Knight Sec may collect information that you voluntarily submit through a contact or audit request form. This may include:

  • Your first and last name.
  • Your organization name.
  • Your organization email address.
  • The domain name you want reviewed.
  • The service or package you are interested in.
  • Information you include in an optional message field, such as your email provider, marketing platform, CRM, or recent DNS changes.

We may also receive standard technical information generated when you visit the website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referral source, and basic server log information.

02 / Use of Information

Why We Collect It

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry.
  • Confirm the domain and scope of a requested audit.
  • Prepare proposals, invoices, reports, and service communications.
  • Provide SPF, DKIM, DMARC, DNS health, and domain-configuration review services.
  • Prevent spam, fraud, abuse, or misuse of our forms and systems.
  • Maintain business, accounting, and service records.

Cyber Knight Sec does not sell your personal information.

03 / Retention

How Long We Retain It

Inquiry and contact-form information may be retained for up to 24 months after the last meaningful communication, unless a longer period is necessary for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or contractual purposes.

Client records related to completed services, payments, reports, and invoices may be retained for up to seven years where reasonably necessary for business and recordkeeping obligations.

Information that is no longer needed may be deleted, anonymized, or securely archived.

04 / Access

Who Can Access It

Access is limited to individuals and service providers who need the information to operate the business or deliver the requested service. This may include:

  • Authorized personnel operating Cyber Knight Sec.
  • Website hosting, form-processing, email, invoicing, payment, and cloud-storage providers.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, attorneys, or insurers when reasonably necessary.
  • Government or regulatory authorities when disclosure is legally required.

Service providers are expected to process information only for the purpose of providing their services.

05 / Deletion

How to Request Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your inquiry by contacting Cyber Knight Sec.

Email: engage@cyberknightsec.com
Suggested subject line: Privacy Request

To protect against unauthorized requests, we may ask you to confirm the email address or organization associated with the original submission. Some information may be retained where required for legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, or security purposes.

06 / Security

How We Protect Information

Cyber Knight Sec uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect submitted information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no website, email system, or online storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card numbers, private encryption keys, confidential employee data, or other highly sensitive information through the public contact form.

07 / Service Conditions

Terms and Service Conditions

By requesting or purchasing a Cyber Knight Sec service, you acknowledge and agree to the following conditions:

  • The Starter Email Authentication Audit is primarily a read-only review of publicly available DNS and email-authentication records.
  • The audit may include SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX, TXT, DNS health, domain configuration, provider identification, and a plain-English executive report.
  • You represent that you are authorized to request an assessment of the submitted domain.
  • The Starter Audit does not include penetration testing, exploitation, inbox access, password testing, malware analysis, internal account review, employee monitoring, or guaranteed identification of every security risk.
  • DKIM findings may depend on selectors supplied by the client or selectors commonly associated with identified providers. A public review may not identify every active DKIM selector.
  • Recommendations are based on the information available at the time of review and should be validated against the organization’s actual list of approved email services.
  • No DNS record should be changed without approval from the domain owner or authorized administrator.
  • Client delays, incomplete provider information, DNS propagation, vendor limitations, and third-party outages may affect delivery or implementation timelines.
  • Payment terms, project scope, delivery date, and any implementation assistance will be confirmed before work begins.
  • Unless otherwise agreed in writing, audit fees cover the assessment and report only. Ongoing monitoring, remediation, and provider coordination are separate services.
08 / Limitations

No Guarantee of Complete Protection

Cyber Knight Sec provides risk findings and recommendations based on the agreed scope. No audit can guarantee that a domain will never be spoofed, that every phishing message will be blocked, that every legitimate email will be delivered, or that every security weakness will be identified.

The effectiveness of SPF, DKIM, and DMARC also depends on correct configuration by email providers, third-party platforms, DNS administrators, and receiving mail systems.
