Starter Audit Scope

What the Audit Covers — and What It Does Not

The Starter Email Authentication Audit is a focused, read-only review of publicly available domain and email-authentication records. This page defines the exact boundaries of the service.

01 / Overview

Audit Scope

The Starter Email Authentication Audit is designed to evaluate whether a domain has the basic public controls needed to support legitimate email delivery and reduce straightforward domain spoofing risk.

The assessment is based primarily on public DNS records, commonly used DKIM selectors, mail-routing information, and details supplied by the client. It is a passive review and does not require access to mailboxes, employee accounts, internal networks, or private administrative systems.

02 / Included

What the Starter Audit Includes

Included

SPF Review

Checks whether an SPF record exists, whether duplicate records are present, and whether the visible policy structure appears valid.

Included

DKIM Review

Checks client-provided or commonly associated selectors for a published DKIM public key.

Included

DMARC Review

Reviews policy level, reporting addresses, percentage, and visible alignment settings.

Included

DNS Health

Reviews relevant MX, TXT, nameserver, and authentication record resolution.

Included

Domain Configuration

Identifies visible mail providers, authorized sending platforms, and configuration issues that may affect authentication.

Included

Executive Report

Provides plain-English findings, a consistent score, risk priorities, recommendations, and stated limitations.

03 / Not Included

What the Starter Audit Does Not Include

The following activities are outside the Starter Audit and will not be performed unless separately scoped, authorized, and priced in writing.

Excluded

Penetration Testing

No scanning, exploitation, intrusion simulation, vulnerability exploitation, or attempts to bypass security controls are included.

Excluded

Inbox Access

No employee, shared, executive, or administrative mailbox will be accessed, reviewed, searched, or monitored.

Excluded

Internal Account Review

No review of user permissions, MFA settings, account activity, admin roles, offboarding status, or internal access controls is included.

Excluded

Password Inspection

No passwords, password hashes, reset records, credential databases, breach dumps, or personal credentials will be requested or examined.

Excluded

Employee Monitoring

No employee behavior, browsing, messages, devices, productivity, communications, or security activity will be monitored.

Excluded

Active Exploitation

No attempt will be made to prove a vulnerability by taking over a subdomain, spoofing a live message, accessing a system, or triggering a security event.

Excluded

Guaranteed Spoofing Prevention

The audit does not guarantee that all spoofed messages, phishing attempts, lookalike domains, display-name attacks, or vendor impersonation will be prevented.

04 / Deliverables

What the Client Receives

  • A written Email Authentication Audit report.
  • SPF, DKIM, DMARC, DNS health, and domain-configuration findings.
  • An overall score based on the published scoring model.
  • Plain-English explanations of why each finding matters.
  • Prioritized recommendations for next steps.
  • Documentation of important limitations and assumptions.

Unless otherwise stated in writing, implementation, DNS editing, ongoing monitoring, and third-party vendor coordination are not included in the Starter Audit price.

05 / Limitations

Important Limitations

Public DNS information provides only a limited view of an organization’s security posture. The audit cannot determine whether employee accounts are compromised, whether internal mail rules are malicious, whether MFA is enabled, whether credentials are reused, or whether a private system has been breached.

DKIM results may be incomplete when the client does not provide active selectors or when a vendor uses selectors that are not publicly predictable.

The effectiveness of SPF, DKIM, and DMARC depends on the accuracy of the organization’s sender inventory and the behavior of third-party mail platforms and receiving providers.

06 / Authorization

Client Approval and Authority

The client must be authorized to request an assessment of the submitted domain. The Starter Audit is read-only and does not require credentials or administrator access.

No DNS record will be created, changed, replaced, or deleted without approval from the domain owner or an authorized administrator. Any implementation work must be separately confirmed in writing.

07 / Separate Services

Work Requiring a Separate Agreement

Services beyond the Starter Audit may be available under a separate written scope. Examples include:

  • DNS remediation and implementation assistance.
  • DMARC monitoring and staged enforcement.
  • Email-provider or vendor coordination.
  • Internal account and MFA review.
  • Security awareness or phishing-resistance training.
  • Authorized vulnerability assessment or penetration testing.
